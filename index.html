About

A camera in hand,
a story in every frame.

Shot_byTeni is a London-based photography practice founded by Teni — a photographer with a fresh, youthful eye and a documentary instinct for real moments.

Every session is built around the person in front of the lens: relaxed, unscripted, and shaped to reflect genuine personality rather than a formula. From portraits to lifestyle content, brand shoots and creative concepts, the goal is the same — images that still feel true a decade from now.

"Let's bring your vision to life, one shot at a time."

Booking & Session Policy

Please read before booking your session.

  1. Booking confirmation. A non-refundable payment of £10 is required to confirm your booking — no appointment is secured without it.
  2. Late arrivals. Arriving more than 20 minutes late adds a £5 late fee to your final balance.
  3. Rescheduling. Free of charge for emergencies or weather-related issues — please give as much notice as possible.
  4. Image delivery. Standard turnaround is 2 days. Expedited delivery is available on request for an additional fee.

By booking with Shot_byTeni, you agree to these terms. Questions are always welcome before you book.
